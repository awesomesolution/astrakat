OUR LEADERSHIP

Pratiksha Renake

Head of Design

Pratiksha Renake leads our design team with creativity, precision, and a deep understanding of space and aesthetics.

With over 12 years of experience in interior architecture and bespoke furniture design, Pratiksha combines creativity, innovation, and precision to transform ideas into beautifully crafted living spaces.

A Master’s in Architecture (MArch) graduate from University College London (UCL), she is passionate about designing interiors that are timeless, functional, and uniquely tailored to each client. Her visionary approach, attention to detail, and commitment to excellence ensure every project is both inspiring and practical.

“Great design isn’t just about how a space looks - it’s about how it makes you feel.”

UCL University College London
