Privacy Policy

We value your trust and are here to help! If you have any questions or concerns about our Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, feel free to reach out. Let's connect and build something meaningful together.

This Privacy Policy describes how AstraKAT Designs Limited (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you contact us via our website (astrakatdesigns.com) contact form, interact with our Facebook or Instagram presence (including through lead generation ads), or contact us via WhatsApp or Google Ads.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with applicable data protection laws, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. We are based in the United Kingdom.

1. Information We Collect

We collect different types of information depending on how you interact with us

Website Contact Form

When you submit a contact form through our website, we collect the information you provide, which may include your name, email address, phone number, address, project details, and any other information you choose to share.

Facebook & Instagram Lead Ads

When you submit a lead generation form through a Facebook or Instagram ad, we collect the information you provide, which may include your name, email address, phone number, address, project details, and any other information you choose to share.

Facebook & Instagram Interactions

When you interact with our Facebook or Instagram pages such as liking, commenting, or sharing our posts Facebook/Instagram may provide us with certain information about you, depending on your privacy settings. We only collect information you actively provide to us via lead ads or direct messages.

WhatsApp

When you contact us via WhatsApp, we collect the information you provide during the conversation, which may include your name, phone number, and any other information you choose to share.

Google Ads

When you interact with our Google Ads, Google may provide us with certain information such as which ad you clicked on and your general location. If you fill out a form linked from a Google Ad, we collect the information you provide in that form.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes

Responding to Inquiries

To respond to your inquiries submitted through our website, social media, WhatsApp, or Google Ads, provide quotes, and discuss your project requirements.

Providing Our Services

If you become a customer, we use the information you provide to manage your project, communicate with you, and fulfil design and installation services.

Marketing and Communication

With your explicit consent (where required under GDPR), we may use your information to send you marketing communications about our interior design services, bespoke furniture, and promotions. You can opt out at any time by contacting us or clicking the unsubscribe link in our emails. We only use information from contact forms, WhatsApp, or ads for marketing if you specifically agree to this.

Legal Compliance

We may use your information to comply with applicable laws and regulations.

Facebook/Instagram Lead Ads

Information collected via lead ads is used solely to contact you regarding your interior design inquiry, provide quotes, and manage your project if you become a client.

WhatsApp

Information collected through WhatsApp is used to communicate about your inquiry, provide design consultations, and manage your project. We only use WhatsApp for marketing with your explicit consent.

Google Ads

Information collected through Google Ads is used to respond to your inquiries and provide quotes. We will only use this information for marketing with your consent.

3. Sharing Your Information

We may share your information with the following third parties

Service Providers

We may share your data with trusted third-party service providers who assist with customer management, marketing (where you’ve consented), cloud storage, or business operations. These providers are contractually required to protect your information and use it only for the purposes we specify.

Legal Authorities

We may disclose your information to authorities where required by law or legal process.

Facebook/Instagram (Lead Ads)

Data collected through these platforms is shared with Facebook/Instagram to deliver our ad campaigns and receive leads. Their processing of your data is governed by their respective privacy policies.

WhatsApp

We use WhatsApp Business. Your communications and data are subject to WhatsApp’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Google (Ads)

Information collected through Google Ads is shared with Google to deliver ad campaigns and manage inquiries. Google’s use of your data is governed by their privacy policy.

4. Your Rights (Under GDPR)

As a UK resident, you have the following rights regarding your personal information

  • Access: Request access to the data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure (“Right to be Forgotten”): Request deletion of your personal data under certain circumstances.
  • Restriction: Request restriction of data processing under certain conditions.
  • Data Portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to processing for direct marketing purposes.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting previous lawful processing.

To exercise your rights, please contact us using the details below.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected — such as responding to inquiries, providing design services, or meeting legal requirements. We regularly review our retention policies to ensure GDPR compliance.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our website and services are not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Effective Date.”

Deposit and Cancellation Policy

Once a deposit has been received, AstraKAT Designs Limited allocates significant professional time and resources to the project. This may include client consultations, site visits, measurements, surveys, preparation and revision of 3D designs, material selection, technical planning and detailed drawings for production and on-site contractors.

If the client chooses to cancel the project, the following terms will apply:

  • Before any 3D designs have been shared: 100% of the deposit will be refunded.
  • After 3D designs and detailed drawings have been prepared or shared: 50% of the deposit will be retained to cover the design work, consultations, revisions, and resources already provided. The remaining 50% will be refunded.
  • After the site survey has been completed, multiple visits or design revisions have taken place, or the project has reached the final preparation stage before production or installation: 70% of the deposit will be retained to cover the professional services, surveys, drawings, project planning and resources already committed. The remaining 30% will be refunded.

The applicable deduction will be based on the stage reached and the work completed when the written cancellation request is received. Where the reasonable costs already incurred exceed the applicable deduction, AstraKAT Designs Limited reserves the right to provide the client with an itemised calculation, subject to the agreement and applicable law.
